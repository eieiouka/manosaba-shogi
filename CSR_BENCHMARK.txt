CSR edge-storage benchmark

Measured in this session with the same rules and symmetry canonicalization.

200,000 discovered positions:
  v6 packed graph:
    about 4.52 s, 110 MB RSS, 57 MB heap
  v7 CSR graph:
    4.33 s, 96.7 MB RSS, 41.0 MB heap

1,000,000 discovered positions:
  v6 packed graph:
    24.4 s, 216 MB RSS, 153 MB heap
  v7 CSR graph:
    23.1 s, 218 MB RSS, 132.9 MB heap

The main gain is lower JS object/heap overhead:
- no per-node successor arrays
- one flat successor buffer
- flat reverse-predecessor CSR for retrograde solving
- typed-array result, distance, degree, and queue storage

At one million positions, graph generation is still incomplete; no opening move
is claimed best from this benchmark.
