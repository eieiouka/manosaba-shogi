Symmetry benchmark (same rules engine, this session)

Fixed number of expanded positions:

10,000 expanded
  without symmetry: 81,943 unique positions
  with symmetry:    78,105 unique positions
  reduction:         4.68%

30,000 expanded
  without symmetry: 206,803 unique positions
  with symmetry:    199,110 unique positions
  reduction:          3.72%

60,000 expanded
  without symmetry: 394,633 unique positions
  with symmetry:    384,022 unique positions
  reduction:          2.69%

The symmetry is useful, but it is not a magic 2x reduction because the search
frontier is not itself perfectly symmetric at shallow depth. It will be kept as
one exact deduplication layer rather than relied on as the main speedup.

v5 also changes exact graph keys so they use the complete compact position
encoding. The 64-bit hash remains only an optional pre-hash; exact W/D/L results
do not rely on collision-free hashing.
