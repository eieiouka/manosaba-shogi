Manosaba Shogi exact research status

This version adds:
- shared rules engine fixes used by the solver
- exact reachable-position graph builder
- retrograde W/D/L solver
- principal-variation extraction

Important rule-engine fixes included:
- Emma is never actually capturable; attacks on Emma are used only for check legality.
- Nanoka cannot promote merely by shooting.
- duplicate drops of identical held piece types are removed from move generation.
- promoted Sherry's retained two-square forward move can capture on the landing square if the intermediate square is empty.

Exact solving method:
1. Enumerate every reachable position from the initial state, merging identical positions.
2. Mark terminal losses.
3. Retrograde:
   - a position is WIN if it has any move to LOSS;
   - a position is LOSS if all moves go to WIN;
   - positions left in cycles after propagation are DRAW.
4. Store distance-to-result so a winning side selects the shortest forced win and a losing side delays defeat.
5. Extract the principal variation from the initial position.

Prototype benchmark in this session:
- Initial position: 15 legal moves.
- A graph-building test reached about 989,000 unique positions after expanding about 150,000 positions.
- The complete reachable graph was therefore not yet exhausted in that benchmark.
- No best opening move is claimed until the root position is solved exactly.

Only a completed graph + retrograde result should be treated as '読み切り'.
